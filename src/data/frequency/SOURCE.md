# Frequency lists

The 35 000 most frequent words per language, one per line (line number = rank), from
**FrequencyWords** by Hermit Dave, built from the OpenSubtitles 2018 corpus:
https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords (`content/2018/{en,fr,es}/*_50k.txt`).

Content license: CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
Changes: counts removed, lowercased, non-word entries dropped, cut to the top 35 000.
