# French lexicon

Part of speech and noun gender for ~24,500 French lemmas from **Lexique 3.83**
(New, Pallier, Brysbaert & Ferrand 2004; http://www.lexique.org), CC BY-SA 4.0.
Lemmas used at least 0.3 times per million words (films + books). Built by
`scripts/build-lexicon.mjs` (format described at its top).
