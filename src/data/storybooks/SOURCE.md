# Easy illustrated stories

Short stories for beginners, page by page with a picture, read aloud by a native speaker, from
**Global Storybooks** (https://globalstorybooks.net, University of British Columbia; stories largely
from the African Storybook): https://global-asp.github.io/storybooks-french/ and
https://global-asp.github.io/storybooks-spanish/. The same story's Turkish edition on the same site
gives each page's Turkish.

Licence: **CC BY 3.0 / CC BY 4.0**, as stated on each story's page (kept in `license`); stories under
a non-commercial licence are left out. Each story keeps its writer, illustrator, translator and
reader (`credit`), shown with the text in the app. Audio and pictures stay on Global Storybooks'
servers (linked, not copied).

Built by `scripts/build-storybooks.ts` from the stories listed in `docs/kaynaklar/storybooks.json`
that have a recording: French 29, Spanish 29, all with Turkish.
Changes: title page dropped (it is the title), text and Turkish taken page by page, HTML removed.
